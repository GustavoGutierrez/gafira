# Gafira name and logo

Gafira's source code is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (see `LICENSE`). The license covers the code. It does not grant rights to the Gafira name, logo or brand.

## What you can do

- Say that your work is based on Gafira, uses Gafira or is compatible with Gafira.
- Use the name and logo to talk about the official project, for example in an article, a review, a tutorial or a link to <https://gafira.com>.
- Build, modify and share the code under the GPL.

## What needs permission

- Distributing a modified version under the name Gafira, or with the Gafira logo or app icon. A fork must use its own name and icon.
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests the official project made, endorses or supports your product, service or package.
- Using the name or logo in your own product name, domain or company name.

## Brand files and illustration packs

The logos, app icons and other files in `docs/manuals/marca/assets/` and `assets/app-icon/` identify the official project; they are not covered by the GPL grant of this file. Illustration packs (`.gafpack`) sold or given away by the project carry their own license inside each pack.

To ask for permission, open an issue in the repository.
