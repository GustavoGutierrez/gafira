### Choices and deviations

- **The stages are cumulative by construction**: the budget stores the instant its frame started and derives every deadline as `start + offset`; no method reads the clock again, so a stage that runs into the margin cannot move the deadlines of the frame. H1 is the "independent budgets" mutation, and the test of AC1 also advances the scripted clock between the budget and the deadline to pin that the instant is the start.
- **The equality rule has one seam, in the loop**: `deadline_passed` delegates to `ports::Deadline::has_passed` (the port owns the arithmetic), and the loop calls it before every tile. H2 and H3 are applied on that seam, so the boundary the crate enforces is the code the mutants move.
- **One tile per `execute` call**: the loop of 02 §6.3 drives one task of the `RenderPlan` per call, which is what lets it check the cancel token and report the progress at every tile boundary and stop exactly at the deadline; the backend also receives the deadline and the token for its own check. A backend that batches several tiles per call is a later optimisation, and it would have to give the loop the same boundaries through the port.
- **`FrameRequest` instead of eight arguments**: the request carries the clock, the target, the deadline, the cache, the cancel token and the progress; clippy's `too_many_arguments` is why, and the frame call stays at the request plus the plan.
- **The degradation triggers on `RenderError::DeviceLost` only** (ARQ-22, 02 §6.4), from `begin_frame` or from `execute`, and the GPU is dropped. No GPU at construction is reported as `Degradation::AdapterMissing`; a GPU `OutOfMemory` is not stated to degrade the session, so the selector keeps the GPU for it. The selector never creates a backend (P10).
- **"The document survives" is structural in this crate**: the loop receives a plan and a cache, never the document, so the failure cannot touch it. The ARQ-22 test builds a page and its graph, loses the device on the second task of the plan, and asserts the graph's key and the node count are unchanged; the edit-session half of ARQ-22 (the next edit applies and the UI stays editable) is the engine's, and the real-driver case waits for `gafira-render-gpu` (design, "Out, and why").
- **The best mip available is the level of the last plan that finished**: `FrameLoop` keeps `presented`, a late frame reports that level and `refine`, and the next frame continues the plan the caller submits; the cache of `RE3` is what makes the finished tiles free. The loop does not own the plan across frames: the caller resubmits it (cheap to rebuild, ARQ-140) and the outcome says what to present meanwhile.
- **The progress is per frame**: the fraction is the tiles of this plan done in this frame over the tiles of the frame, so the caller hands a frame-scoped `Progress`; the contract of ARQ-112 checks the token before every report, and the fraction reaches `1.0` only when the frame finished the plan.
- **ARQ-41 and the `<= 100 ms` of ARQ-43 are proved as a shape**: the 16 ms arithmetic, the deadline contract and the per-tile cancel are unit behaviour; their §6.7 measurements and benches stay with the recorded debt, and this delivery adds no bench ("Critical code" does not mark the benches of `RE4`).
- **ARQ-143 is the product row this budget implements**, although the Slice card names ARQ-22, ARQ-41 and ARQ-43: the tests name the card's IDs, so `git grep -n 'ARQ-22\|ARQ-41\|ARQ-43'` finds them.
- **No shared file and no dependency**: the change is three files of `gafira-render`; `Cargo.toml`, `Cargo.lock`, `gafira-ports`, `gafira-doc`, the testkit and the `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` of `lib.rs` are untouched.

### Choices and deviations

- **The rejection of an out-of-range page is the scope mapping's, as the amended card decides.** `PageScope::resolve` checks every page against the document and answers `ScopeError::OutOfRange { page, pages }` before a render exists (FD-58). `PageRenderer::render` stays total: a page the document does not hold answers the empty region of the request, in the format of the quality, because the port fixes no variant for it, `Cancelled` would tell the user they cancelled and `DeviceLost`/`AdapterMissing` would degrade the session — the two harms the card names. An exporter that skips the mapping gets no pixels and fails in its own buffer check.
- **`render` answers the region, not the page.** The output is `region.width() × region.height()` — the same shape as the double of ARQ-112 and the media exporter's `region` — and `scale` only picks the level of detail of the preview (`lod = level_for(scale)`), the export rendering at the level zero. The output size of FD-55 and the resampling of another scale stay with the exporter and with the O3 backend, as the card records. A region with an offset is planned correctly (the tiles clip to it) but the offset itself is not carried to the backend: the plan has no viewport transform yet, and the selection export that needs it is layer 4.
- **The quality is taken at construction and decides two things only**: the pixel format of the surface (`Rgba8` premultiplied sRGB, `RgbaF32` linear) and the zoom of the plan (the request's `scale` for the preview, 100 % for the export). The graph, its nodes and their operations are the same for both; the quality reaches them only as the `KeyParam::Quality` of the content key. `GraphPageRenderer::quality` answers what the renderer was built for.
- **The page background is an operation, not a post-process.** Without `transparent`, the renderer prepends a `FillPath` of the page in `PAGE_BACKGROUND` (white, opaque, the default of FD-55) to the operations of every tile, so the backend paints it under the content and clips it to the tile. With `transparent`, no operation is added and the surface keeps its alpha. The page model has no background colour field yet, so white is the only value; a page with its own colour gives it to the exporter, which paints it the same way.
- **The render runs to the end.** The port gives `render` no deadline, so the frame is `Deadline::Unbounded` and an export is never cut by the 16 ms budget of the preview (02 §6.2). The instant still comes from an injected `Clock`: the renderer takes one at construction and never creates one, and with `Unbounded` no instant it answers can change the pixels (AC6).
- **`FrameLoop::read_back` is the seam `RE4` was missing.** The loop already opened the surface and ran the tasks in it; it now keeps the handle and answers a region of it, and the renderer is its first caller. `None` (no frame ran) is answered with the empty region, so the render stays total; the deadline, the cancel per tile and the progress of `RE4` do not change.
- **The renderer owns a `Mutex` around the loop and around the cache** because `PageRenderer::render` takes `&self` while the loop and the cache need `&mut`. Poisoning is recovered (`PoisonError::into_inner`), never unwrapped: a panicking backend must not turn a later render into a panic.
- **The graph build runs on the calling thread (`InlinePool`).** The plan is one page and the tile work is the backend's, which owns the pool of the session (ARQ-138). `ARQ-140` is what keeps the result independent of that choice.
- **`RE3`'s cache is shared across renders**: the renderer is built with a `ContentTileCache` and hands it to every frame, so a tile a page rendered is free on the next one (AC1's test stores one tile and reads it back).
- **ARQ-42's golden travels with O3**: the 1/255 comparison on the 99.9 % of the pixels needs the CPU backend (`gafira-render-cpu`), which is a different change. What this delivery proves is the shape the golden depends on: one graph, one set of operations, the quality only in the content keys and in the format.
- **`gafira-ports`, `gafira-doc`, the testkit and `gafira-render`'s `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` are untouched**; `Cargo.toml` and `Cargo.lock` do not change, and the delivery adds no dependency.
- **ARQ-42 and FD-57/58 are the product rows this delivery implements**; the tests name the card's IDs, so `git grep -n 'ARQ-42\|FD-57\|FD-58'` finds them.
